About Oven Fresh

A passion for food, a love for craft

Where Bakery Meets Restaurant

Founded in 2020, Oven Fresh was born from a simple idea: why choose between artisan baked goods and fine dining when you can have both? Our founders, a master baker and a classically trained chef, joined forces to create a unique culinary experience.

Every morning, our ovens come alive with the aroma of fresh breads and pastries. As the day progresses, our kitchen transforms into a gourmet restaurant serving carefully crafted dishes that celebrate the finest ingredients.

🌾 Quality First

❤️ Made with Love

Every item is crafted with passion and care.
